Welcome to Kolhapur Tourism

We are a Kolhapur-based tour management company offering planned tours within the city and nearby destinations.

For many years we have served pilgrims, families, and visitors who wish to see Kolhapur in a simple, organised manner. Our packages follow fixed routes and timings so you always know where you will go and how long each stop will take.

Kolhapur is known across India for the Mahalaxmi Temple, its royal history, wrestling tradition, Kolhapuri footwear, and the reformist vision of Rajarshi Shahu Maharaj. We help you experience this heritage with respect and comfort.

Legacy of Kolhapur Maharajas

Kolhapur & Rajarshi Shahu Maharaj

Kolhapur has been a centre of Maratha royalty, learning, and social reform. Rajarshi Shahu Maharaj (1874–1922) is remembered as a progressive ruler who worked for education, upliftment of backward communities, and modern administration in the princely state of Kolhapur.

When you travel with us, you walk through the same city that shaped this history — from royal buildings and museums to temples that have welcomed devotees for centuries.
