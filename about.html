Who We Are

Kolhapur Tourism is a tour management company based in Kolhapur, Maharashtra. We organise group and family tours to important places within Kolhapur city and to nearby hill stations, forts, temples, and wildlife areas.

Our strength is fixed tour packages — each package has a set route, schedule, and inclusions. This traditional, straightforward approach helps guests, especially those visiting for the first time, to plan with confidence.

Historical Kolhapur

Kolhapur’s history spans ancient temples, medieval fortifications, and the Maratha period. The city is the seat of the Bhonsle dynasty of Kolhapur and remains a major pilgrimage centre because of the Shri Mahalaxmi (Ambabai) Temple.

The New Palace (Shahu Maharaj Palace), museums, Rankala Lake, and old city lanes together tell the story of a living heritage — not only monuments, but festivals, cuisine, and crafts such as the famous Kolhapuri chappal.

Rajarshi Shahu Maharaj

Rajarshi Shahu Maharaj ruled the Kolhapur state from 1894 to 1922. He is honoured as a social reformer who promoted education, reservation for backward classes, and humane governance. Institutions and public works from his era still shape Kolhapur’s identity.

Visitors interested in history often combine temple darshan with a visit to palace and museum areas to understand how Kolhapur connected tradition with progress — a theme we are proud to share on our heritage tours.
