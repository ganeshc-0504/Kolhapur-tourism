In Kolhapur City

These destinations are commonly covered in our city and heritage packages.

Shri Mahalaxmi Temple

One of the Shakti Peethas; central to Kolhapur pilgrimage. Dress modestly; follow temple rules for photography and darshan queues.

Rankala Lake

Historic lake with peaceful surroundings — popular for evening walks and family outings after temple visits.

New Palace (Shahu Maharaj Palace)

Royal residence associated with the Kolhapur Bhonsle rulers; architecture and museum collections reflect Maratha-era grandeur.

Old City & Markets

Traditional bazaar, Kolhapuri chappal shops, and local food — part of the living culture of the city.

Near Kolhapur

Panhala Fort

Hill fort with Maratha history and wide Sahyadri views — about 20 km from Kolhapur.

Jyotiba Temple

Hilltop temple dedicated to Jyotiba — busy on weekends and festival days; included in our weekend packages.

Gaganbawda & Amba Ghat

Scenic Western Ghat routes — popular for monsoon travel and nature photography.

Radhanagari Wildlife Sanctuary

Forest and wildlife experience; visits depend on season and sanctuary timings.
